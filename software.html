Software

Software

Codes I have contributed to and codes I use in my research. My repositories are on GitHub.

§ Contributed

Codes I have contributed to

  • pymcpop-gw

    Hierarchical population and cosmology inference with HMC in PyMC. Building on its earlier formulation, I developed the mixed discrete–continuous inference strategy used for the dark-siren host ranking.

  • GWFish

    Fisher-matrix forecasts for gravitational-wave detectors. In my M.Sc. thesis I extended it to test the nature of compact objects and horizon-scale physics at Einstein Telescope sensitivity.

§ Used

Codes I use

  • icarogw

    Standard-siren pipeline, one of those pushed to the third-generation regime in the mock data challenge.

  • chimera

    Standard-siren pipeline, also part of the third-generation mock data challenge.

  • GWFAST

    Fisher forecasts for detector networks, used in the Einstein Telescope design-study taskforce.

  • posterior-landscape

    Morphological feature finding in two-dimensional probability densities, presented in arXiv:2610.40272 (co-author) and used in the mass-plane study.
